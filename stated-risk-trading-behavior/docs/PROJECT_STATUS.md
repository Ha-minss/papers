## External access limitation

The official ATHEX Composite Share Price Index and the Bank of Greece historical workbook were identified and documented. The binary daily workbook could not be downloaded inside the isolated runtime. The package therefore contains a source manifest and multiple internal market-proxy validations rather than falsely claiming an official-index rerun.

Corporate-action linkage is high-confidence but not exhaustive for every cash distribution and issuer event. The paper retains the neutral term `extreme negative-return event` and does not claim exogenous shocks or causal effects.
