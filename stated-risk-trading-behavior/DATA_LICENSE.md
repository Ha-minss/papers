# Data licensing and attribution

The original FAR-Trans dataset is not redistributed in this repository. It is available from its original publisher under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license.

The included derived datasets were constructed from FAR-Trans for research reproducibility. Users must retain the original source attribution and comply with the source dataset license.

## Required source citation

Javier Sanz-Cruzado, Nikolaos Droukas, and Richard McCreadie, “FAR-Trans: An Investment Dataset for Financial Asset Recommendation,” IJCAI 2024 Workshop on Recommender Systems in Finance, 2024.
